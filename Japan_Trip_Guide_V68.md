## How to add a new location (checklist for future edits)

When a place is added to this guide, all of the following must happen:

1. **Route calculator** — add an entry to `RC_LOCS` with real coordinates (look them
   up, don't estimate) and a Chinese label.
2. **Day rows** — add it to *every* day it realistically fits, not just the first.
   Each row states: address, hours, a 📍 route line (what it's near / how far /
   which side), a one-line verdict, then TO BUY / TO EAT / GOOD TO KNOW bullets.
3. **If it lands inside Day 6's Shibuya hour** — tag the row with
   `data-lane="east|west|sw|ne"` so the lane picker filters it, and update that
   lane's chain chips and its walking total.
4. **If it lands inside Day 7's ALT PLAN** — add it to the step it belongs to as a
   `.spare` box, stating the time cost of the detour.
5. **Cross-check every distance against the route calculator** and fix the prose if
   they disagree.
6. **Both languages** — the EN body and the `data-zh` attribute.
7. If it sells something on the shopping list, add it to that item's `where` field
   and bump `SL_SEED`.
8. Bump `GUIDE_VERSION` so the version badge reflects the change.

## ✈ Airport & customs rules: Japan → Singapore → Malaysia
**1 · Leaving Japan (Narita, D8)**
- Carry-on liquids: max 100ml per bottle in one 1L clear bag → Seika ZEN and big bottles must already be **checked**
- **Tax-free sealed bags:** keep sealed in Japan; customs can inspect at departure, an opened consumables bag can mean paying the 10% back
- Power banks & lithium batteries: **hand-carry only**
- Royce'/chocolate with ice packs: fine in hand-carry if bought airside; gel packs from outside may be taken at screening

**2 · Transit in Singapore (2h15m)**
- You stay **airside — no immigration or customs**, so SG allowances don't apply
- Changi re-screens carry-ons at the gate: liquor bought at **Narita** survives only in the sealed STEB bag with the receipt inside, unopened
- **Simpler:** buy liquor **at Changi during transit** — straight onto the final flight, no extra screening risk, usually cheaper
- Jewel is landside — skip it, 2h15m isn't enough

**3 · Arriving Malaysia (Penang)**
- Alcohol: **1 litre per adult** (non-Muslim, 18+) duty-free — one bottle is safe, more must be declared
- **Cigarettes: no duty-free allowance** anymore — declare any amount
- Other new goods: roughly **RM500** exemption per person
- ⚠ **Meat warning:** gyutan jerky and meat-filled snacks need import permits and are commonly **seized** — eat them in Japan. Fresh fruit also restricted. Packaged sweets, chocolate, cakes and cosmetics are all fine.

**Airport must-buys (Narita T1, airside):** Royce' Nama chocolate (buy last, cold pack, hand-carry) · Japan-only KitKats · Shiroi Koibito & Tokyo Banana · duty-free Japanese whisky/sake/umeshu · airport 7-Eleven for cheap snack souvenirs.

## 🇯🇵 Japan rules & etiquette
**In public:** stand **left** on escalators (Tokyo/Tohoku) · no eating while walking in shopping streets — eat standing by the stall · almost **no public bins**, carry a small trash bag · smoke only in marked areas · don't jaywalk.
**Trains, buses, shops:** phones on silent, no calls · queue at the marked lines, let people off first · **no tipping** · put cash on the small tray · alcohol & tobacco age is **20**.
**Ryokan & onsen (4 onsen nights):** shoes off at entrances, never slippers on tatami · wash and rinse fully **before** entering, hair tied up · no towels in the water (fold the small one on your head) · tattoos — ask the front desk, many hotels have private baths · yukata **left side over right**.
**Trip-specific:** Zao Fox Village — don't crouch, don't dangle straps or phones, no touching · shrines — bow at the torii, don't walk dead-centre, no photos where marked · Shibuya Crossing — film while walking, don't stop mid-crossing · earthquake — drop, cover your head, follow hotel staff, don't run outside.
