What this order drops: 3COINS LUMINE EST, Ikedaya Tea, Blue Bottle and HANDS all shut at 9pm and sit south near the station — you can have those or dinner at Gyukatsu, not both. Swap dinner for a konbini bento and the southern four become possible.
Morning instead? Most of these open 10–11am — but your Day 7 coach leaves Shinjuku early for Tokyo Tower, so there’s no morning window unless you skip something.
Reading the map: the shops cluster in two places — Kabukichō in the north (Godzilla, 3COINS+plus, Ichiran, Donki, Gyukatsu) and the station in the south (Onitsuka, OS Drug, Bic, LUMINE EST). Ikedaya, Blue Bottle and HANDS are further south again, which is why they’re the first things to fall off.

Ginza — add-on to the LOFT stop

Five places, all within 10 minutes of each other and of Ginza LOFT, which is already in your Day 7 plan. This is an add-on, not a separate trip.

Key Coffee and Ippodo are 40 m apart, in the same Mitsukoshi basement. That’s one stop, not two.
Ippodo is the one to do first. Reviewers queue from before the 10am opening and tins sell out; the limit has moved between 2 and 5 per person, and tin sizes shrank from 40g to 20g.
Blue Bottle opens at 8am, two and a half hours before GINZA SIX. If you’re in Ginza early, enter from the subway level — one reviewer found the mall shut and had to go round via the metro passage.
Glitch shuts at 6pm, two hours before the rest — so if you want it, it goes first. It’s also the furthest east, about 10 min from GINZA SIX.
