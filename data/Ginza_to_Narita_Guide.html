Ginza → Narita Airport, on your own

If you skip Shisui on Day 7 and stay in Ginza: how to get yourself to the airport for the hotel shuttle — and how to come back with a receipt you can claim.

Which route Step by step When to leave

Which route

The catch: the tour reimburses a JR ticket. The direct train from Higashi-Ginza is cheap and needs no change, but it’s Keisei/Toei — not JR.

Step by step

1Decide before you leave the shopsClaimable or convenient — not both▾

The tour covers one JR ticket. The direct train from Higashi-Ginza is Keisei/Toei, not JR — cheaper and no change, but probably not claimable.

Pick: walk to Tokyo Station and take the JR Sōbu Rapid — about the same money and time as the direct train, but it can be claimed back.
❗ Confirm with the guide whether “the hotel station” means Narita Airport station or Narita station — they are different places and different tickets.
2Ginza → Tokyo Station17 min on foot, or one Marunouchi stop▾

Walk north up Chūō-dōri from Ginza 4-chome — about 1.4 km, 17 min, shops the whole way. With bags, take the Marunouchi line one stop: ~3 min, ¥180.

  • Tokyo Station is huge — follow the green JR signs and “総武地下 / Narita”.
  • The Sōbu platforms are five floors underground and a further 8–10 min walk from the gates — budget for it.

3Buy at the counter, not the machineThis is the step the claim depends on▾

Go to the Midori-no-madoguchi (JR ticket window) or the JR EAST Travel Service Center, buy a ticket to Narita Airport, and ask for the ryōshūsho.

Say this: 「成田空港まで、大人一枚。領収書をお願いします。」
Narita kūkō made, otona ichimai. Ryōshūsho o onegai shimasu.
(To Narita Airport, one adult. Receipt please.)
  • A ticket stub is not a receipt. The ryōshūsho is a separate slip with the amount and date on it
  • Machines can print one too, but you must press 領収書 on screen before the transaction ends — miss it and it’s gone
  • Photograph it immediately, then hand the paper to whoever is claiming
  • Buying for four? Say otona yonmai — but ask for separate receipts, or only one person can claim
TO FILLPhoto of the ticket window, and of the ryōshūsho itself so everyone knows what they’re looking for.
4Find the Sōbu Rapid platformUnderground, platforms 1–4▾

Follow the 総武線（地下） signs down. You want a Rapid bound for Narita Airport — not a local.

  • The same platform serves trains to Narita (city) and to Yokosuka — check the destination board says Narita Airport before boarding
  • Some trains split at Sakura or Narita — sit in the front carriages, which are the airport portion
  • Last two stops are Airport Terminal 2 then Narita Airport (T1)

5Get off at the right terminal, then the shuttleWhich terminal depends on your hotel▾

From the platform go up to the departures level; the hotel shuttles leave from numbered bays outside the terminal.

  • The two possible hotels use different terminals and bays: Yurakujo T2-33 / T1-16; Marroad T1-16 / T2-27
  • Full timetables for both are in the N’EX guide under References
  • Miss the last bus and it’s a taxi, about ¥2,500–3,500

So for the 17:30 / 17:35 shuttle, leave Ginza around 15:10 — 15:00 if you want slack.
Rush-hour Sōbu Rapids get crowded and have limited luggage space — with big bags, N’EX is worth the difference.
