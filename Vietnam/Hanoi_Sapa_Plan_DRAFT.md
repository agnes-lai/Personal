## Book or arrange before you go

1. 🔴 **Pizza 4P's** — you flagged this. Reviews mention an hour's wait even with the
   e-queue system, so book the 18:00 slot.
2. 🔴 **HK sleeper bus**, both directions. Confirm the Sapa drop-off point and the
   Hanoi pick-up point — they are not always the same place.
3. **Fansipan combo ticket** (funicular + cable car) — buy at Sun Plaza, or online ahead.
4. **Grab** installed and signed in before you fly.
5. **Day 1 luggage** — ask Jovie's Pullman whether they'll hold bags for a non-guest. If
   not, Old Quarter cafes store them for about 50,000₫.
6. **Cash** — street food and the cốm shop will be cash only.

## The alternatives, with where and when

**Day 4 lunch — pick one:**

| | Where | Hours | Why you'd pick it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Quán Ăn Ngon** | 18 Phan Bội Châu | **06:30–22:00** | Michelin. Food-stall format under one roof — lots of dishes in one sitting. 4.4★ from 6,310. Busiest of the three |
| **Hanoi Garden** | 36 Hàng Mành | ⚠ **11:00–14:00, 17:00–22:00** | Michelin. Courtyard setting, private rooms, calmer. 4.3★. **Shuts 14:00–17:00**, so it's lunch or dinner, not in between |

**Lan Ông** is a street, not a restaurant — the herbal/apothecary street in the Old Quarter.
It's on the Day 1 walk between Bánh Mì 25 and Bún Chả Ta, so you pass through it anyway.

**Coffee beans — two different things:**

| | Where | Hours | |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Café Huệ** | 26 Hàng Giầy | 08:00–22:00 | ☕ **They brew it, so you can taste before buying.** Weasel coffee, egg coffee. 4.8★ |
| **Huệ cafe** | 103 Hàng Gai | 07:30–20:30 | Beans only, no tasting. Same family, more central. 4.8★ |
| **Là Việt** | 26 Châu Long | 07:30–22:00 | Đà Lạt roaster, gift packaging. 4.7★. North — pairs with Kem Caramen |

⚠ A reviewer at the Hàng Giầy branch notes explicitly that the one near the Cathedral
**doesn't brew**, so you can't taste before you buy. If tasting matters, go to Hàng Giầy.

**Supermarket:** **BRGMart Hàng Trống**, 63 Hàng Trống, 07:00–22:00, 4.0★ — two floors
with the snacks upstairs, 3 min from the cathedral. I'd avoid the WinMart+ on Tràng Thi:
3.4★ with repeated reviews about being overcharged and refused a receipt.
